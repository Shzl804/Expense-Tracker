Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information.

Data We Collect

Spendly may collect information you provide when creating an account, such as your name and email address. We also store the expense details you choose to record, including amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions.

We may receive limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as browser and request information.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve Spendly. Your expense data helps us display your records, summaries, and spending patterns to you.

We may also use account and technical information to respond to requests, protect the service, and communicate important service updates.

Data Storage

Your account and expense information is stored so you can access it through Spendly. We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Please keep your login details private and let us know if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party hosting, infrastructure, or other services needed to operate the application. Those providers may process limited information on our behalf and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not share it except as needed to provide the service, comply with the law, or protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the support channel provided with the service.
